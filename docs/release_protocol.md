# Release gates and change control

## Gate 1. Method and input provenance

- Preserve hashes of the original two-sheet source and all intermediate workbooks.
- Store the exact seed, candidate row order, 48-to-24 crosswalk and stage scripts.
- Confirm reference replay has zero differences among 630 assigned IDs and arm labels.

## Gate 2. Geographic certification — CGA

- Confirm official wards, fix spelling variants, validate cross-county entries.
- Explain any grouping that remains operational rather than administrative.
- For corrections that change operational stratum membership, obtain statistical sign-off and determine whether a new randomization is required.

## Gate 3. Eligibility certification — CGA and iShamba

- Resolve the 39 actionable selected-person cases (15 urgent digital-arm).
- Retain different farmers sharing phones; verify whether a farmer already exposed to relevant digital advisories is wrongly present.
- Verify any duplicate-registration disagreements (FSC, youth, crop) before reporting final category counts.
- Keep a row-specific decision log with evidence, reviewer, date, action, and effect on the sampling-frame version.

## Gate 4. Data-sharing and contact authorization — designated project authority

- Confirm NDA/data-sharing framework, purpose/consent/notification/opt-out requirements and access controls.
- Share only the authorized PSP+iShamba contact list with the delivery partner; the private combined audit workbook remains restricted.
- All currently assigned farmer contacts are on HOLD; a code-generated Excel workbook is not contact authorization.

## Gate 5. Final reporting

- Label the current sample as **provisional** until approvals are signed.
- Any revised sample must be versioned as a separate run and linked back to the original assignment; never overwrite history.
- Methods/results report covers **sampling and randomization only**. Nyandarua and impact analysis are explicitly out of scope.
